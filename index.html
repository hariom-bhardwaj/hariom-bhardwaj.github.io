I'm Hari Om, an Electronics & Communication Engineering student specializing in embedded systems and IoT. I bridge the gap between hardware and software, turning circuit diagrams and firmware into projects that actually hold up outside the lab.

My approach pairs rigorous academic training with hands-on prototyping — signal processing, embedded programming, and circuit design working together to solve real engineering problems, not just textbook ones.

Off the clock, I'm usually exploring new hardware, contributing to open-source electronics, or mentoring engineers earlier in their journey.
